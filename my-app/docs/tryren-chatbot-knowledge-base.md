## SECTION 1: ABOUT TRYREN

**Q: What is Tryren?**
A: Tryren is a weekly matchmaking app for college students and young professionals in India. Instead of endless swiping, we hand-pick one meaningful match for you every week — based on your personality, interests, and what you're looking for.

**Q: Is Tryren a dating app?**
A: Tryren is a matchmaking platform, not a swipe app. You can join for dating (finding a relationship or something casual) or for BFF mode (making new friends). Either way, you get one curated match per week.

**Q: Who can join Tryren?**
A: Anyone can join — college students and non-students alike. Students are matched with students from their campus or nearby colleges. Non-students are matched based on their city and area.

**Q: Is Tryren available as a mobile app?**
A: Tryren is a web app — just open tryren.in in your phone's browser. No download needed. You can add it to your home screen for an app-like experience.

**Q: Is Tryren on the Play Store or App Store?**

## SECTION 2: SIGNING UP & ONBOARDING

**Q: How do I sign up?**
A: Visit tryren.in, enter your phone number or email, verify with the OTP we send, and complete your onboarding profile. The whole thing takes about 5–10 minutes.

**Q: I didn't receive my OTP. What should I do?**
A: First, check if you entered the correct number. Then wait 30 seconds and request a new OTP. If you're still not receiving it, try using your email instead. If the problem persists, message us here and we'll help.

**Q: What information do I need to complete onboarding?**
A: Your name, date of birth, gender identity, what you're looking for (dating or friendship), who you'd like to meet, your height, interests, personality traits, and at least one photo.

**Q: Why do I have to complete onboarding before using the app?**
A: We want every profile on Tryren to be real and complete. Incomplete profiles make for bad matches — so we ask everyone to finish onboarding before they can access the app.

**Q: I completed onboarding but can't access the app. What's wrong?**

**Q: Can I edit my profile after onboarding?**

## SECTION 3: HOW MATCHMAKING WORKS

**Q: How does matchmaking work on Tryren?**
A: Each week, you opt in to matchmaking before Friday midnight IST. On Saturday morning, our AI agents analyse all opted-in profiles and select the best match for you based on your interests, personality, preferences, and location. You receive your match via email on Saturday.

**Q: When do I need to opt in to get a match this week?**
A: You must opt in before Friday midnight IST. Opt-ins after that deadline will be considered for the following week.

**Q: When will I receive my match?**
A: Matches are sent out on Saturday morning (IST) via email.

**Q: How is my match selected?**
A: Our AI agents analyse all opted-in profiles every week. They consider your gender preference, age, location, interests, personality, and what you're looking for — then pair you with the person they think you'll connect with the most.

**Q: I opted in but didn't get a match. Why?**
A: This can happen if there weren't enough compatible profiles in the pool that week (e.g., not enough people of your preferred gender in your area opted in). We'll always try our best, but we can't guarantee a match every single week.

**Q: How do I receive my match?**
A: You'll get an email with your match's details (name, a few things about them, and how to reach them). Check your inbox and spam folder on Saturday.

**Q: What happens after I receive my match?**
A: You can reach out to them directly using the contact details in the match email. The first move is yours — say hello!

**Q: Can I choose who I get matched with?**
A: No. Tryren is an AI-powered matchmaking service — our AI agents select the best match for you. You set your preferences (gender, age range) and the AI does the rest.

**Q: What if I don't opt in this week?**
A: No problem — you simply won't be matched that week. You can opt in again next week.

**Q: How many matches do I get per week?**
A: One. We believe one thoughtful match is more valuable than many random ones.

**Q: Can I opt out of matchmaking?**
A: Yes — just don't opt in for that week. Opting in is always your choice, week by week.

**Q: Are students matched only with students from the same college?**
A: We prioritise same-college matches, but if there aren't compatible profiles from your college, we may match you with someone from a nearby campus.

**Q: Are non-students matched differently?**
A: Yes. Non-students are matched based on their city and sub-area (neighbourhood), not college. You'll be matched with VIP non-students who opted in from your area.

**Q: What is the VIP plan?**

A: VIP is billed monthly at ₹99. Within that month you can receive up to 4 matches — one per week, every week you choose to opt in. You're not charged per match; the monthly plan gives you access to all 4 weekly slots.

**Q: How much does VIP cost?**

**Q: Why is matchmaking only for VIP members?**
